# GreyBox Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

GreyBox is a Windows app for arranging the files, folders, photos, links and notes on your PC into project boards. This policy explains what information GreyBox handles and why.

## The short version

- Your boards, files and notes stay on your PC. GreyBox does not upload them.
- An account is optional. If you create one, GreyBox stores your name, email address and profile picture so it can sign you in.
- GreyBox has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

## Information that stays on your PC

Everything you put on a board stays in your Documents folder (`Documents\GreyBox`) and GreyBox's settings folder on your PC: the boards, the paths of the files and folders you link, copied photos, thumbnails, notes and settings. None of it is sent to us.

## Accounts (optional)

You can use GreyBox without an account. If you choose to create one or sign in, we receive:

- **With Google (or another sign-in provider such as Apple):** your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID from that provider. GreyBox does not get your password for that provider or access to anything else in your account with them.
- **With email and password:** the name (if you give one), email address and password you choose. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in service.

We use this information only to:

- create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in;
- show who is signed in, inside the app;
- send emails about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.

Account information is stored by [Supabase](https://supabase.com), the service GreyBox uses for sign-in. On your PC, the sign-in is saved in GreyBox's settings folder, encrypted so that only your Windows user account can read it. Signing out removes it from your PC.

### Information from Google

GreyBox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We use the name, email address and profile picture Google provides only to sign you in to GreyBox. We do not use them for advertising, do not sell them, and do not share them except as described in this policy.

## Syncing

GreyBox does not sync your boards yet. Before it does, this policy will be updated to explain what is stored online and how, and syncing will be something you choose to turn on.

## Other connections GreyBox makes

- **Update checks:** GreyBox checks GitHub (github.com) for new versions. GitHub receives the usual information any website gets, such as your IP address.
- **Link previews:** when you add a web link to a board, GreyBox loads that page to show its title and a preview picture. The website receives the usual information any visitor sends.
- **AI assistants:** GreyBox has no AI built in. If you connect an AI app to GreyBox, it connects on your own PC, and that app's own privacy policy applies to what it does.

## Who we share information with

We share account information only with the services needed to run GreyBox's sign-in: Supabase, and the sign-in provider you choose (such as Google). We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell your information.

## Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. To delete your account and the information stored with it, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

## Children

GreyBox is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
